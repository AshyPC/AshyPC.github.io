## Prayers for our World and the Church 

### 15th November 2026

**Psalm 33:16-22**
_[16] The king is not saved by his great army; a warrior is not delivered by his great strength._
_[17] The war horse is a false hope for salvation, and by its great might it cannot rescue._
_[18] Behold, the eye of the Lord is on those who fear Him, on those who hope in His steadfast love,_
_[19] that He may deliver their soul from death and keep them alive in famine._

Let us pray…

**Thanks to God**
O Lord, we thank You for David, the man after Your own heart, who was a shepherd, a prophet and a king over Your people Israel. We thank You that he understood that we are not saved by what we can do in this world, but by You alone.

Like David, we confess that we are sinners in this fallen but redeemable world. We thank You that we have been forgiven by Your grace and mercy alone, and have been set free from sin. You gave Jesus, Your only Son, Who willingly went to the Cross to take our sin and Who then rose to life again to make us righteous in Your sight, and to secure our eternal salvation.

We thank You for Your Presence with us, and that You hear and answer when we call out to You.

**The Wide World... and the Wider Church**
We thank You Lord, for Your Church where we learn to live according to Your Word, growing in grace and living as ambassadors of the Kingdom of heaven. We pray that we might respond eagerly to the leading of your Holy Spirit as He works in our hearts to manifest the grace, mercy and peace that this world needs in abundance.

We pray for our State and our nation at this election season, thanking You that we have opportunity to elect our parliamentary representatives. We pray that whatever the eventual outcome, we would apply Godly wisdom in the casting of our vote, recognising that we are citizens of this state but also servants of the King of kings, and that our votes matter. May we fully grasp the importance of our love for our neighbours, and our longing for Biblically based righteousness in the government of our communities.

**Ashburton**
We pray for our work here at Ashburton, as we labour with our various talents and abilities in the work of the Kingdom. We thank You for all who work in this place, taking up various tasks even whilst we seek a new full-time servant-minister of the gospel to lead the work here. We pray that we may welcome new people, enable everyone to grow to spiritual maturity, and minister to one another within and beyond our four walls. 

We continue in prayer for those in need of Your healing hand – for Levi, John and others affected by illness or injury. 

We thank You for Your Word, and for Brett as he preaches today. May his words be applicable to our particular circumstances, so that more will come to know Your love as we show to the world that we have been transformed by the daily renewing work of the Holy Spirit.

In Jesus’ Precious Name we pray, Amen.
