**Sermon Outline**

“THE LORD WHO KEEPS YOU”   (Luke 23:13-25 & Psalm 121:1-8)

Introduction

1. Where does your help come from?
2. It comes from the LORD because:
   1. He's our covenant God and creator (Luke 23:13-25)
   2. He’s our keeper
      1. He never tires (vv3-4)
      2. He closely shields us (vv5-6)
      3. He promises to keep us forever (vv7-8)
3. Our LORD keeps us through Christ
4. Behold the LORD who keeps us

Conclusion

**Call to Worship (Isaiah 44:1-3) & Opening prayer** 

_[1] “But now hear, O Jacob my servant, Israel whom I have chosen!_
_[2] Thus says the Lord who made you, who formed you from the womb and will help you:_ 
_Fear not, O Jacob my servant, Jeshurun whom I have chosen._
_[3] For I will pour water on the thirsty land, and streams on the dry ground;_
_I will pour my Spirit upon your offspring, and my blessing on your descendants._

Let us pray…
Heavenly Father, we have gathered together today in response to Your call. We know that You have made us, chosen us, and called us as Your servants, and so we glorify You this day and every day. You are the One in Whom we can trust, the One Who dispels all our fears. We honour You Who commands the heavens to bring rain upon dry ground, but also the One Whose Word is living water to satisfy our thirsty souls, too.
We thank You for Your mercy in forgiving our sins, for pouring out Your Spirit upon us and our children, and for the blessing of the New Life we live by the eternal power of the Risen Lord. We pray that in our gathering today all we do and say would bring glory to Your Name, Amen.
